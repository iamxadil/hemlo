# Salary & social-security calculator · حاسبة الراتب والضمان

**Status:** next.

**Why:** we found no interactive Iraqi calculator; there are only articles. Employers need, every month:

- the net pay for each employee,
- the employer's real cost, and
- the social-security (Daman) amounts.

**v1 scope:**

- **Inputs:** gross salary (IQD), allowances, and whether the employee is registered with social security.
- **Outputs:**
  - the employee deduction (5%), the employer contribution and the net pay,
  - the total monthly cost to the employer, and
  - a printable payslip (Arabic/English).
- **Team mode:** list several employees and get a monthly payroll summary as a PDF.

**Rules:**

- Every rate must be checked against the current law and the MOLSA / Tax Authority instructions before launch.
- Results are labelled as estimates. The tool gives no legal or tax advice.
