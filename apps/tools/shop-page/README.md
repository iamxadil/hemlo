# Free one-page site for a shop · صفحة محلك مجاناً

**Status:** later. It needs light storage (Cloudflare KV/D1 free tier).

**Why:** it's the most shareable hook. Every shop posts its link, and every visitor sees "Made with Hemlo".

**v1 scope:**

- **Page content:** name, category, logo and cover photo, WhatsApp and call buttons, a location pin with a landmark description, working hours, and 3–6 photos. Arabic and Kurdish.
- **Address:** hemlohq.com/s/shop-name, plus a printable QR sticker for the counter.
- **Owner access:** the owner edits it with a phone-number code, with no password.

**Before building:** search for Iraqi competitors first.
