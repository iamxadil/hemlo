The one hard correction. An earlier draft priced Solo at 32,000 and Clinic at 79,000 IQD. Research on Ivor's published rates showed the Iraqi dental market anchors much lower than assumed — 250,000/year, not 384,000. The cards above are revised downward from that first pass. Losing 4,500 IQD/month per Solo shop is the right trade for a price the market will actually accept.

Not in these numbers. Payment processing (FIB takes a cut), support time, and sales effort. Those are real costs and none of them scale with server count — which is the argument for price discipline rather than discounting.

6 · Open decisions

  • Confirm the live USD→IQD rate. Everything here uses 1,450 as a planning figure; Ivor's own published IQD pricing is the more reliable anchor.
  • Decide whether FIB processing fees are absorbed or passed through. At 30,000 IQD/month, ~2% is 600 IQD — enough to notice.
  • Set the trial length. TRIAL_DAYS = 7 is currently in the code; 14 days may convert better in a market where trials are expected.
  • Confirm the annual discount is 10 months across all three tiers before it appears in public copy.
